Top Engineering Colleges in AP

A quick look at some of the most popular and well-regarded engineering institutions across Andhra Pradesh.

1
JNTUK College of Engineering, Kakinada

📍 Kakinada

A premier autonomous government institution and one of the oldest engineering colleges in AP, known for strong core branches and research culture.

2
Andhra University College of Engineering

📍 Visakhapatnam

A century-old institution with a strong legacy in engineering education, well-equipped labs and an active alumni network.

3
VIT-AP University

📍 Amaravati

A modern private campus known for industry tie-ups, strong placement records and a focus on emerging tech specializations.

4
Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation (KLEF)

📍 Vaddeswaram, Guntur

A deemed-to-be university with NAAC A++ accreditation, large campus and a wide range of engineering and research programs.

5
SRM University-AP

📍 Amaravati

Part of the well-known SRM group, offering modern infrastructure and collaborations with global universities.

6
GITAM (Deemed University)

📍 Visakhapatnam

A large deemed university with multiple schools of engineering, known for research output and international collaborations.

7
VR Siddhartha Engineering College

📍 Vijayawada

A well-established autonomous college known for consistent placements and strong alumni presence in the IT industry.

8
RVR & JC College of Engineering

📍 Guntur

An autonomous institution recognized for academic discipline, good infrastructure and steady placement performance.

9
Sagi Rama Krishnam Raju (SRKR) Engineering College

📍 Bhimavaram

A reputed autonomous college in the West Godavari region, known for its civil and mechanical engineering programs.

10
Aditya Engineering College

📍 Surampalem, East Godavari

A growing private institution with modern facilities and an increasing focus on placement training and skill development.

Rankings are a general guide — always verify latest official NIRF / NAAC rankings before applying.
